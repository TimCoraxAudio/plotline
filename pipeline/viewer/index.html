Plotline Chain viewer: check steps and links

Pick a chain

Search by chain or quest name, or by quest ID, or filter by zone. Each chain opens as a list of steps, with a card for each quest at that step.

Each card's links show every source that claims a quest leads to or from another. A struck-through red link was removed by the splitter, with the rule that removed it. A link marked turn-in goes to a reward quest that's kept with the chain but isn't a step.
